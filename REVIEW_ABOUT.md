# Review: About and Methodology pages

New and rewritten text for the About page (/about/), the new Methodology & sources page (/methodology/), the footer link, the calculator's "Where the typical amounts come from" link and the email line on the Contact page. Each string is in `src/i18n/locales/<language>.json` under the key shown.

Please check that each translation reads naturally and says the same as the English. Keep these unchanged:

- **SpendMath**, **Google AdSense** and **hello@spendmath.com** stay in Latin script.
- Link markup such as `<a href="/methodology/">…</a>`: translate only the words between the tags.
- `{date}` must stay as it is; the page fills in the date.

Points to look at:

- **The owner's name** is written as محمد اسحاق (Urdu), मुहम्मद इसहाक़ (Hindi), محمد إسحاق (Arabic) and Muhammad Ishaq (Spanish). Confirm the spelling the owner prefers.
- **First person.** The English switches from "SpendMath is designed and run by Muhammad Ishaq" to "I design…". The translations do the same; check that the switch reads naturally.
- **"Methodology"** is طریقۂ کار (ur), कार्यप्रणाली (hi), المنهجية (ar) and metodología (es).
- **US Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Expenditure Survey.** Urdu and Hindi transliterate it, Arabic translates it, and Spanish translates it with the English name in brackets.
- **Arabic "Get in touch"** is «تواصل معي» (first person, like the rest of the page).

The page titles (`about.seo.title`, `methodology.seo.title`) must not be the same as the H1; `npm run i18n:check` fails if they are.

French, Portuguese, Indonesian and German are switched off, so they show this text in English until they're translated.
